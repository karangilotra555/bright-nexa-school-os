3. WordPress admissions integration

An authorized WordPress administrator may save a Bright Nexa school connection key in WordPress settings and publish the [bright_nexa_admissions] shortcode on a school page. The plugin forwards a submitted parent's or guardian's contact details and optional grade information, together with the school key, to the connected school's secure Bright Nexa inbox. The visitor must check the consent box to submit. An enquiry is not a confirmed admission or an automatically enrolled student. The school, not Bright Nexa, decides how to follow up and whether to admit anyone.

To disable submissions, clear the connection key in WordPress, unpublish the form, or revoke the key within Bright Nexa. Use HTTPS and exclude live forms from full-page caching where required for WordPress security tokens. Administrators remain responsible for their public website and any disclosures there.
