Connect an actual school

Create an owner account for your school, verify your email and open Full School OS. If you use WordPress, generate a connection key in WordPress Admissions, save it in the plugin and add [bright_nexa_admissions] to your admissions page. Real admission enquiries will appear in your school’s inbox when parents submit the form with consent.

Test the whole School OS

Use a separate owner account with an email address you control to create a separate test school. Open Full School OS, add two classes, a fictional student in each and a test teacher assigned to one class. Use fictional records and contact details you control for test logins. Open the Android app download page for Teacher and Student access and the Parent Portal for linked family access.

School setup, admissions and documents
  • Save a school profile and add classes, sections and students. Refresh the Dashboard and open each student profile to verify the changes.
  • Connect a separate test WordPress site to your test school and keep your live WordPress site connected to your actual school. Submit a practice enquiry on the test site and mark it reviewed in WordPress Admissions. Create a student record separately to test enrolment.
  • Open ID Cards & Certificates and preview the documents for the correct test student.
Staff, timetable and teaching
  • Invite the test teacher, activate the account and grant attendance, homework or marks permissions for one class.
  • Publish a timetable and confirm the assigned teacher can see the right class and periods in the Android app.
  • Mark student attendance, publish homework and enter exam marks where permitted. Reopen the app to confirm the saved entries.
Family access and published records
  • Set up the test student’s family login and verify that the Parent Portal or Student app shows only their linked student.
  • Publish a notice and a marksheet. Check the linked account for attendance, homework, published results, fee balance and notices.
  • Submit a practice leave request and check that it reaches the school. Use test recipients for any messages you send.
Fees, expenses and reports
  • Set a test fee, record a fictional payment and check the updated balance. Refresh and verify that there is no duplicate receipt.
  • Try a concession or reversal in the test workspace. Verify the amount due in the owner’s reports and the linked family account.
  • Record a fictional expense, check the budget and compare the totals in Owner Reports with the entries you saved.
Staff operations, transport and assets
  • Add non-teaching staff and test staff attendance in Full School OS. Submit leave as the test teacher and review it in the Leadership portal.
  • Add a sample route and asset. Assign the route to a test student and confirm both records are visible after refresh.
Teaching tools, progress and school quality
  • Try AI Teacher Tools and Smart Tools with fictional classroom examples. Review the output before using it in your test school.
  • Open Opportunity Desk, Growth Passport and School Quality Score. Check that each screen reflects the information available to the signed-in role.
Sync, permissions and recovery
  • Save a test record on one device and open the same school on another. Verify the saved record and try an offline edit followed by reconnecting.
  • Switch the test teacher and family accounts to confirm class and student access. If you test Accountant or Librarian access, verify the modules the owner has enabled for each account.
  • Try backup and recovery only in the test workspace. Reopen the workspace and verify the selected school and records before continuing.
